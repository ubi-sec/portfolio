Put these two files in this folder:

1. resume.pdf   — your resume, exported as a PDF.
                  This is what the "Download résumé" and "View in browser"
                  buttons link to.

2. profile.jpg  — optional headshot. If you add one, open index.html,
                  find the "about-visual" section, and replace:

                      <span class="avatar-initials" data-cfg="initials">YN</span>

                  with:

                      <img src="assets/profile.jpg" alt="Portrait of Your Name">

If you don't add a resume.pdf, the download buttons will simply lead to
a 404 until you do — everything else on the site works without it.

You can delete this file once you've added your own.
