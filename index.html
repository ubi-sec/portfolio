About me

I'm an entry-level cybersecurity professional building a practical foundation in security operations, systems administration and risk management. My background combines formal study with self-directed lab work: standing up virtual networks, triaging simulated alerts, and writing the kind of documentation a SOC or GRC team actually relies on.

I'm most interested in the point where technical detection work meets the process side of security — reading logs and packet captures, but also translating findings into risk language that a business can act on. I learn quickly, document everything I do, and I'm comfortable being the newest person in the room as long as I'm useful in it.

Analytical by habit

Comfortable working through logs, alerts and evidence methodically before drawing conclusions.

Always learning
